## Note on the employer-credential stat

The 75% employer-credential stat in `siteContent.js` (`EVIDENCE_CARDS`, id
`employer-stat`) is attributed to Ma et al., 2025. During verification, the
stat's ultimate primary origin turned out to be Coursera's *Global Skills
Report 2025*, which found 75% of employers "express a preference for
GenAI-proficient talent even over those with more traditional experience."

The group has decided to keep the Ma et al., 2025 attribution rather than
add Coursera as a seventh source, since Ma et al. is already one of the six
sources required by the assignment. This is a deliberate scope decision —
worth knowing if it ever comes up, but not something to change in the build.
